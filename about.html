Our Story

A legacy of faith, hope, and love in Central Texas

Killeen New Hope Seventh-day Adventist Church has been a beacon of faith and hope in the Killeen community for decades. Founded by a small group of dedicated believers, our church has grown into a vibrant, diverse family of worshippers united in our love for God and service to others.

Located near Fort Cavazos (formerly Fort Hood), we have the unique privilege of ministering to military families, veterans, and the broader Killeen community. Our church family includes people from all walks of lifemilitary and civilian, young and old, long-time Adventists and those new to faith.

Through the years, we've remained committed to our mission: sharing God's love, proclaiming the everlasting gospel, and preparing hearts for Christ's soon return. We've weathered challenges, celebrated victories, and witnessed countless lives transformed by God's grace.

Today, New Hope continues to be a place where people find acceptance, discover purpose, and grow spiritually. We're not perfect, but we're a family striving together to live out our faith authentically and make a positive impact in our community.

Our Beliefs

What We Believe and Why It Matters

Core Beliefs

As Seventh-day Adventists, we hold to the fundamental beliefs of Christianity while emphasizing certain biblical truths that shape our identity and mission.

  • The Bible: God's inspired Word and our ultimate authority for faith and practice
  • The Trinity: One God in three personsFather, Son, and Holy Spirit
  • Salvation by Grace: Eternal life through faith in Jesus Christ alone
  • The Sabbath: Saturday as God's holy day of rest and worship
  • Second Coming: Christ's literal, visible return to earth
  • Whole Person Health: Physical, mental, and spiritual wellness
  • Service: Loving God by serving others

What Makes Us Distinctive

Sabbath Observance

We worship on Saturday, the seventh day of the week, in honor of God's creation and as a sign of our covenant relationship with Him. The Sabbath (Friday sunset to Saturday sunset) is a gift of rest and renewal.

Blessed Hope

We joyfully await Christ's soon return. This hope isn't about fear, but anticipationlooking forward to the day when suffering ends, justice prevails, and we're united with our Savior forever.

Whole Person Health

We believe God cares about our total well-being. Our health ministry emphasizes plant-based nutrition, physical activity, mental wellness, and spiritual growth.
