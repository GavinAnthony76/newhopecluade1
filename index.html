Welcome from Our Pastor

Welcome to Killeen New Hope Seventh-day Adventist Church! Whether you're a long-time Adventist, exploring Christianity, or simply seeking a place to belong, we're glad you're here.

Our church family is committed to sharing God's love through worship, fellowship, and service. We believe in the transformative power of Jesus Christ and the hope found in His soon return.

We'd love to meet you this Sabbath and help you discover the abundant life God has planned for you.
